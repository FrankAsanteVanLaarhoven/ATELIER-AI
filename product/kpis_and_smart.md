# SMART Objectives and KPIs

## SMART launch objective
Within 12 weeks, deliver a production pilot that supports diagnostic testing, task/domain drills, a 60-question timed mock, three case-study labs, one capstone workflow, source-versioned content, and an admin analytics dashboard for at least one controlled cohort.

## KPIs
1. Content verification coverage: 100% of live items linked to approved sources and last-verified date.
2. Item quality: ≥0.20 point-biserial discrimination for mature items; review/retire below threshold once sample size is adequate.
3. Distractor efficiency: ≥70% of distractors selected by at least one learner in calibration cohorts; revise dead distractors.
4. Learning lift: ≥15 percentage-point median improvement from diagnostic to final mock within a cohort, reported with sample size and uncertainty.
5. Capstone reliability: ≥95% rubric evidence links resolvable and reproducible by an independent reviewer.
6. Platform reliability: ≥99.9% exam-session API availability during scheduled assessment windows; no lost responses.
7. Governance: 0 released items derived from prohibited exam dumps or unverifiable “real question” sources.

## SWOT
Strengths: public blueprint alignment; practical labs; source lineage; agent/harness creation; enterprise analytics.  
Weaknesses: no access to Anthropic’s private item bank or official scoring-equating function; seed bank needs calibration data.  
Opportunities: enterprise Claude adoption, role-based upskilling, custom organization skills/plugins, recurring recertification.  
Threats: syllabus/product changes, unlicensed branding claims, question leakage, rapidly changing Claude interfaces, low-quality AI-generated competitors.
